Maison Thierry Stravers

Perfumery as Poetry

Founded in Paris in 2018, Thierry Stravers began as a singular obsession: to compose fragrances that transcend trend and speak directly to emotion.

Every bottle in our collection is the result of months — sometimes years — of sourcing, testing, and refining. We work exclusively with natural and sustainably harvested raw materials, partnering with growers across Morocco, India, Madagascar, and Provence.

Our perfumes are not worn. They are inhabited.

before the moment has happened." — Thierry Stravers
